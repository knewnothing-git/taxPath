## What users still struggle with

Filing products generally optimise data capture, computation, and submission. A citizen still has to decide what matters, distinguish salary documents from wider reported income, understand why a deduction question is relevant, and reconcile records before taking a consequential step.

## TaxPath’s difference

TaxPath is a pre-filing advocate: **understand → investigate → explain → protect → hand off**. Its differentiators are progressive questioning, explanation on demand, conservative benefit language, reconciliation, concrete readiness, and the option to stop a citizen from filing too soon.
