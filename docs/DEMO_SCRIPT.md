# Two-minute demo script

## 0:00–1:00 — citizen experience

“Tax filing apps tend to start with forms. TaxPath starts by understanding the citizen.”

Open the home page. Select **Start my tax check** and choose the highlighted synthetic **Form 16**. After the short parse, show the understood income and TDS. Click **Let’s check**.

Open **Why are you asking?** on the NPS question: “TaxPath explains why it needs each answer, instead of making the citizen guess.” Select **Yes**.

Show **Potential tax impact**, then **Show me why**. “This is not a promise; it clearly shows a prototype estimate and what needs verification.”

Open **Check my filing readiness**. Highlight the TDS mismatch and the final interaction: **Don’t file yet.** “Rather than rushing the citizen to file, TaxPath shows exactly what to review.”

## 1:00–2:00 — what we built and why

“TaxPath is a synthetic, browser-based prototype for Build What Moves India—not an Income Tax Department application. It uses fixture scenarios and deterministic calculations to avoid real data and unsafe automation.”

Show the scenario picker. “The default demo has an opportunity and a mismatch; other scenarios show a clean taxpayer and an income item needing review.”

“We deliberately did not build another filing form, real integrations, or a generic chatbot. The product’s contribution is citizen advocacy: understanding, opportunity discovery, plain-language explanation, reconciliation, and a meaningful readiness check.”

Mention dark mode, responsive UI, accessible controls, test coverage, and the competition-safe synthetic-data boundary.
