# TaxPath

**Your tax, without the tax headache.**

TaxPath is an independent, synthetic-data prototype for Build What Moves India. It redesigns the moment before income-tax filing: helping a citizen understand their information, identify possible benefits, find mismatches, and know what to do next.

It is deliberately not a tax-filing service, government application, or tax advisor. Filing is a handoff after a clear readiness check.

## The vertical slice

The default scenario is Priya, a salaried taxpayer. She selects a synthetic Form 16, sees what TaxPath understood, answers one relevant NPS question with an explanation of why it is being asked, sees a clearly labelled potential impact, then finds a ₹10,000 TDS mismatch between synthetic Form 16 and AIS records. The journey ends with **Don’t file yet** and actionable review steps.

All people, employers, documents, values, accounts, and outcomes shown in the prototype are synthetic.
