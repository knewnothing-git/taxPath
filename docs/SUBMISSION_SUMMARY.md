# Submission summary (exactly 250 words)

TaxPath is an independent prototype for Build What Moves India that rethinks the Indian income-tax experience before filing. Today, citizens often arrive with a Form 16, unfamiliar terms, and fear of an expensive mistake. Existing services can import documents, calculate tax, compare regimes, and submit returns. The difficult human question remains: “What matters to me, and what should I do next?”

TaxPath acts as a calm pre-filing guide. It starts by understanding available documents, then asks only questions that could change a citizen’s situation. Every important question includes “Why are you asking?” in plain Indian English, so the citizen understands the benefit behind the request. The prototype uses fully synthetic data and never connects to government systems or requests real credentials.

In the core journey, TaxPath understands a synthetic Form 16, identifies a possible NPS-related benefit, and labels the result as a potential tax impact rather than a confirmed saving. “Show me why” reveals the calculation inputs and assumptions in language a non-expert can follow. TaxPath then reconciles synthetic Form 16 and AIS records, detects a TDS difference, explains why it matters, and gives a simple review checklist.

Its signature moment is not “file now.” It is “Don’t file yet.” A readiness view shows what has been checked and what still needs attention. TaxPath shifts the experience from data entry to citizen advocacy: understand, investigate, explain, protect, then hand off to filing. It genuinely helps busy taxpayers feel: “I didn’t know what I was supposed to do. Now I do.”
