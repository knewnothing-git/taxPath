# Tax rules and prototype assumptions

Last reviewed: 23 August 2026.

## Official source material

- The [Income Tax Department AIS FAQ](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/ais-faq) describes AIS as a comprehensive taxpayer view and provides a facility for feedback.
- The [Income Tax Department AIS access guidance](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/steps-to-access-ais-information-online) confirms AIS is accessed through the e-filing portal.
- The Department’s [salaried-individual guidance](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-3) identifies Form 16 as a salary TDS certificate.

**Official rule / source behaviour:** AIS and Form 26AS should be reviewed as part of accurate reporting; AIS can contain information requiring taxpayer feedback. TaxPath directs a citizen to verify source records, not to alter them in this prototype.

## Prototype assumptions

- The NPS screen uses a visible illustration: ₹50,000 × an assumed 20% marginal rate × 4% cess = ₹10,400.
- This is labelled “Potential tax impact” and is not a tax calculation, eligibility determination, final tax liability, or recommendation to contribute.
- The exact NPS availability, tax regime treatment, deductions, taxpayer profile, and prevailing law must be verified against current official material before any production use.
- The ₹10,000 TDS mismatch is synthetic and demonstrates an investigation workflow only.

## Synthetic data

All taxpayers, employer names, documents, tax values, AIS records, scenarios, and outcomes are fictitious. Never upload a real Form 16, PAN, Aadhaar, bank statement, or portal credential to TaxPath.
